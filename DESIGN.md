## Brand & Style

This design system embodies modern eco-minimalism: an architectural, organic, and grounded aesthetic crafted to bridge municipal/enterprise B2B credibility with consumer-facing sustainable living. It directly reflects the transformation of recycled composite materials (polyaluminum / *polialuminio*) into sleek urban planters, modular recycling stations, and endemic Andean landscape projects.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Regenerative & Grounded:** Rooted in biological stewardship, native Andean flora, and cyclical manufacturing.
- **Architectural & Precision-Driven:** Far from amateur craft; it projects industrial rigor, municipal durability, and engineering reliability.
- **Warm & Tactile:** Earthy terracotta undertones and stone-tinted off-white canvases evoke rich soil, fired ceramics, and natural Andean sunlight.

### Design Movement
Modern Eco-Minimalism with Tactile Underpinnings. It pairs generous editorial white space, structured modular grids, and soft physical radius treatments (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`) with crisp, high-contrast B2B metrics, industrial spec badges, and confident typography.

## Colors

The color palette anchors industrial sustainability into the biological clarity of Quito's high-altitude valleys.

- **Primary (`#1A4329` - Deep Forest Moss):** Symbolizes longevity, dense highland foliage, and structural weight. Used for primary CTAs, primary navigation anchors, header copy, and prominent product cards.
- **Secondary (`#C47C4B` - Warm Andean Terracotta):** Represents clay planters, baked earth, and warmth. Applied to conversion-focused interactive accents, highlight badges, and architectural focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32` - Leaf Sage Green):** Connects active vegetative life, native landscaping, and progress confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#1E2922` - Charcoal Forest Slate):** Deep blackened moss tone replacing synthetic pitch black for high-readability body copy, specs, and tabular figures.
- **Canvas & Surface System:** Off-white natural linen (`#F8FAF6`) creates an open, breathing background, supported by pure `#FFFFFF` elevated cards and `#EFEFE9` muted functional wells.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the soft geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and impact statistics with the structural, high-clarity legibility of **Inter** for B2B product datasheets, urban planning proposals, and long-form narrative.

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings use negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural solidity and precision.
- **Body:** Inter provides high x-height rendering for technical specifications, material circularity data, and mobile readability.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Clean uppercase or semi-bold title casing with gentle tracking (`0.04em`) delivers clarity on material composition chips and eco-credentials.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt modular spacing grid** contained within a max width of `1240px`, enforcing calm, unhurried airiness around physical product imagery and botanical blueprints.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, with section padding ranging from 64px to 96px (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 40px outer container inset.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`space-md`) margins and gutters. Metrics and modular product grids reflow from multi-column rows into clean swipeable carousels or stacked 1-column cards.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Always pair dense technical tables with expanded whitespace on surrounding container cards to maintain an editorial eco-minimal balance.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic artificial drop shadows in favor of **botanical ambient diffusion** and **tonal containment**:

1. **Subtle Organic Bordering:** Cards and structural modules rely on a soft 1px organic perimeter outline (`#DEE5DC`) rather than dark heavy strokes.
2. **Ambient Forest Shadows:** Elevated elements (modals, active dropdowns, floating request quote drawers) use a double-drop tinted shadow:
   - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(26, 67, 41, 0.05)` with `1px solid #DEE5DC`.
   - *Hovered Card:* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(26, 67, 41, 0.09)`.
   - *Floating Modal/Drawer:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(30, 41, 34, 0.16)`.
3. **Tonal Wells:** Technical specs, recycled material composition graphs, and garden blueprints sit inside recessed surfaces filled with `#EFEFE9` and softened corners, reinforcing physical layered tactile depth.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded Level 2** to reflect the rounded radius of thermo-compressed recycled polyaluminum sheets and organic landscape curves:

- **Buttons & Inputs:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius for an intentional, stable, industrial feel.
- **Cards & Modules:** `16px` (`1rem`) to `24px` (`1.5rem`) corner radius to humanize commercial hardware and architectural planters.
- **Pills & Badges:** Full circular pill radii (`9999px`) reserved strictly for sustainability tags, circularity metrics (e.g., "100% Recycled Tetra Pak"), and project status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary B2B Action:** Solid `#1A4329` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Height: `48px`, font: `label-md`. Hover shifts to `#235735` with gentle elevation.
- **Secondary Terracotta Action:** Solid `#C47C4B` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for immediate inquiries (e.g., "Cotizar Mobiliario Urbano").
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** `#1A4329` 1.5px border, transparent background, `#1A4329` text. Hover fills with `#1A4329` at 8% opacity.

### Eco-Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills with a `4px` leading dot indicator.
- Neutral variant: `#EAD7C5` tinted background with `#1E2922` text.
- Environmental impact badge: Leaf green background (`#2E7D32` at 12% opacity) with solid `#2E7D32` typography indicating kg of recycled Tetra Pak diverted from landfills.

### Cards (Product & Landscape Showcases)
- Solid `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #DEE5DC` and `16px` radius.
- Includes a dedicated architectural aspect ratio (4:3 or 16:9) image frame with an internal `12px` border radius, subtle padding, and bottom metadata chips showing modular specs (dimensions, water retention capacity, material durability).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DEE5DC`, text `#1E2922`. Focus state shifts border to 2px solid `#1A4329` with a zero-offset `2px` ambient halo in `#1A4329/15`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom controls with `#1A4329` fill on check, bordered by `#DEE5DC` in unselected state. Checked icons feature sharp botanical checks with smooth transitions.

### Technical Spec Tables (B2B Modular Systems)
- Alternating subtle rows in `#F8FAF6` and `#FFFFFF`. Numeric metrics presented in bold `Inter` with unit labels in muted slate (`#526357`).
